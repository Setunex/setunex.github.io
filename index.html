How we build

🔒

Zero attack surface

No servers, no accounts, no central database — there is nothing to breach. Each install holds only what you put into it, on that device.

✈️

Offline first

Everything works with no connection. The network is an optional convenience, never a requirement.

📅

Minimal data

We collect the least that makes a feature work — for example a birth year instead of a full date of birth.

🚪

Yours to leave

Your data goes in as a file you own and can be cleared from the app at any time. No lock-in, no export request needed.
